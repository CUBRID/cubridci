CUBRID third-party archive protocol

This demo asks whether an immutable archive can safely preserve CMake's complete third-party tree while every CI pod keeps its own writable build directory. Change the shared-storage state, choose a consumer or publisher, and run a build to see the suggested outcome.

This models a proposal, not production behavior. @tw-kang will validate the storage assumptions and implement any accepted design under a valid CUBRIDQA ticket.
